# Acadence product context

## Product

Acadence is a web-based academic collaboration, productivity, and biometric attendance workspace for a Nigerian university. It brings coursework, schedules, course notices, personal task tracking, and fingerprint-verified attendance into one dependable system.

## Audience

- Students checking what is due, where to be, and their official attendance record.
- Lecturers organising courses, enrolled students, coursework, schedules, notices, and attendance sessions.
- Device administrators managing biometric attendance hardware and enrolment mappings.

## Primary jobs

1. Give students one clear daily academic view.
2. Let lecturers prepare and operate a course without jumping between unrelated tools.
3. Make official attendance records understandable and visibly trustworthy.
4. Keep routine academic actions quick on laptop and practical on mobile.

## Product truths

- The implementation contains no artificial intelligence or machine learning.
- Task priority is a transparent deterministic calculation.
- Official attendance is created by authenticated biometric devices, never by the browser.
- Closed attendance sessions determine official percentages.
- The system uses role-based access for students, lecturers, and administrators.
- Africa/Lagos is the product's academic time zone.

## Platform

Responsive web application. Laptop is the primary productivity surface; student flows must remain practical on mobile.

## Voice

Calm, direct, and academic. Use plain verbs and sentence case. State what happened and what the user can do next. Do not use promotional or AI-themed language.

## Experience commitments

- Light theme is the default and only visual theme unless the product scope changes.
- Academic blue means action, selection, focus, or verified information.
- Content hierarchy comes from type, spacing, alignment, and rules rather than decorative cards.
- Loading, empty, error, and disabled states receive the same care as populated states.
- The interface must be easy to demonstrate during a final-year project defence.
